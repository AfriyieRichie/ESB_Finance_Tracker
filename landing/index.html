Features

Everything you need to take control of your money

Multi-currency accounts

Keep each account in its own currency and see everything added up in yours. Live exchange rates update daily, or set your own rate.

Budgets that make sense

Plan your income, spending and savings separately, and see at a glance how much is used and what is left each month.

Savings & investments

Track treasury bills, fixed deposits, stocks, group savings or your own types, with gains and losses worked out for you.

Reminders & alerts

A gentle evening reminder if you forgot to log today, a weekly summary every Sunday, and alerts when a budget is nearly used.

Works offline

No signal? Keep recording. Everything is saved on your phone and syncs automatically when you are back online.

Install on your phone

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to download it from an app store?

No. Open MiAhorro Pocket in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen. It then opens full screen like any other app.

Is my data private?

Your data is stored securely with Google Firebase and is only accessible when signed in to your account. Signing out also removes the copy saved on that device.

Which currencies are supported?

Over 35, including the pound, euro, US dollar, cedi, naira, shilling, rand and CFA franc. Each account can use its own currency.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Once you have signed in on a device, you can view and record transactions offline. Changes sync when you reconnect.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You record transactions yourself, which keeps you aware of every spend and means your bank login is never shared.
