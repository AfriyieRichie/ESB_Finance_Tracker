Budgets

See where every pound, cedi or dollar is going

Budgets are grouped into income, expenses and savings, so you always know how much you have received, spent and put away, and how much is left for the month.

  • Monthly budgets for any category
  • Totals and progress for each group
  • Alerts at 80% and when you go over
Multi-currency

Money in more than one country? No problem.

Choose your main currency and MiAhorro converts everything else into it, so your net worth and budgets are always in one number you understand.

Your history never drifts. Many trackers re-value past transactions at today's exchange rate, so last month's spending changes every time the rate moves. MiAhorro saves the rate on the day you record each transaction, so your history stays exactly as it happened.
  • Over 35 currencies with daily rates
  • See each account in its own currency, with its value in yours
  • Record the exact amount that arrived on transfers, fees included
Account history

Every account's history at a glance

Tap any account to see exactly what came in and went out, for this month, the last 30 days or any dates you choose.

  • Money in, money out and net for the period
  • Transfers shown on both accounts, in each one's currency
  • Newest or oldest first

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to download it from an app store?

No. Open MiAhorro Pocket in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen. It then opens full screen like any other app.

Is my data private?

Your data is stored securely with Google Firebase and is only accessible when signed in to your account. Signing out also removes the copy saved on that device.

Which currencies are supported?

Over 35, including the pound, euro, US dollar, cedi, naira, shilling, rand and CFA franc. Each account can use its own currency.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Once you have signed in on a device, you can view and record transactions offline. Changes sync when you reconnect.

How is this different from other budgeting apps?

It's built for money in more than one currency. Every account keeps its own currency, your totals and budgets are converted into yours, and past transactions keep the exchange rate of the day, so your history doesn't change when rates move.

Isn't logging every transaction a lot of work?

It takes a few seconds per entry, works offline, and a gentle 8pm reminder only appears on days you haven't recorded anything. Many people find that logging by hand is exactly what makes them more aware of their spending.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You record transactions yourself, which keeps you aware of every spend and means your bank login is never shared.
